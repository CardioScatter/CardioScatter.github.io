Overview

CardioScatter is a fully passive continuous ECG system. Instead of harvesting RF energy into a DC supply, it maps millivolt cardiac potentials directly onto a frequency-shifted parametric response that a compact reader can follow and demodulate.

A new sensing path

A passive ECG sensor that never builds a power rail.

Conventional battery-free wearables still harvest and regulate DC power before sensing begins. CardioScatter removes that chain: the cardiac signal itself tunes a passive RF system.

01

The threshold problem

Harvest-first sensing must build a supply before it can observe the body.

Rectification, regulation, sampling, and digital communication create a hard operating threshold: when the power rail collapses, sensing stops.

02

The direct mechanism

Signed cardiac voltage tunes the radio itself.

CardioScatter connects differential ECG to a voltage-dependent resonator, so millivolt cardiac dynamics shift the tag’s analog resonant state directly.

03

The systems payoff

Parametric regeneration moves the weak modulation onto a readable RF response.

Frequency separation lets the reader isolate each tag, track slow drift, and recover continuous ECG without tag-side conversion or logic.

FAQ

The short answers.

Is the on-body tag truly battery-free?

Yes. The tag does not store energy or build a regulated DC supply; ECG directly tunes its passive analog response.

Where does signal processing run?

Tag detection, drift tracking, demodulation, calibration, and ECG reconstruction all run at the off-body reader.

Can one reader recover more than one ECG stream?

Yes. CardioScatter separates concurrent frequency-shifted responses so multiple passive tags can share one RF pump.
